Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

This is the Privacy Policy for "Think English" (the "App"), an English learning application developed by Lepton Apps. Think English is designed to help users improve their English skills through interactive learning features and curated content.
At Lepton Apps, we take user privacy seriously and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the information collected through your use of the App. It also describes the third-party services we use to provide and improve the App. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal information and how we handle it. By creating an account or using Think English, you acknowledge and agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features. The information we collect may include:

- Your email address when you create an account or sign in using Google or other supported authentication methods.
- A unique Firebase Authentication user identifier (UID) associated with your account.
- Purchase tokens associated with in-app purchases. These tokens are used to verify and manage your purchases.
- Remaining credits, which are used to provide and manage the corresponding features of the App.
- Learning-related information, such as your listening scores, generated through your use of the App.

+ Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services:

- Firebase Authentication and Google's authentication services may automatically collect certain technical and security-related information, including IP addresses, device and application metadata, and diagnostic information necessary to provide authentication services and protect against fraudulent or abusive activity.

- Firebase App Check may collect information such as Firebase user agent information and Play Integrity tokens to help verify that requests to our backend services originate from genuine and untampered instances of the App.

- When you watch YouTube videos through the App, Google and YouTube may automatically collect information necessary to provide video playback services. This may include IP addresses, device and WebView information, video playback and interaction data, cookies or other identifiers used by YouTube, and approximate location information derived from your IP address. If you are signed in to your Google or YouTube account, Google may associate your viewing activity with that account in accordance with Google's privacy policies and your account settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To create, authenticate, and manage your account.
- To provide the App's English learning features and save your learning-related information.
- To process and verify in-app purchases and provide the corresponding features and entitlements associated with those purchases.
- To maintain your remaining credits and other information necessary to provide the App's functionality.
- To maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App and its backend services, including preventing fraudulent or abusive activity.
- To provide, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App and its features.

Sharing of Information

We may permit trusted third-party service providers to process information collected through the App when necessary to provide the App's functionality. In particular:

- Information necessary for account authentication and management may be processed by Firebase Authentication and Google's authentication services.
- Information necessary to provide the App's functionality may be processed and stored using Google Cloud services, including Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions.
- Information relating to in-app purchases may be processed for purchase verification and management through Google Play services.
- Information relating to YouTube video playback may be processed by Google and YouTube when you watch videos through the App.

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services provided by Google to provide authentication, backend functionality, security features, purchase verification, and video playback services. These services may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The third-party services used by the App include:

- Firebase Authentication (user authentication and account management).
- Firebase App Check (verification that requests originate from genuine instances of the App).
- Cloud Firestore (secure storage of application data).
- Google Cloud Functions (backend processing necessary to provide the App's functionality).
- Google Play services (Google Sign-In and verification of in-app purchases).
- YouTube services (video playback and related functionality).

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through the App from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocols.
We use trusted third-party services, including Firebase, to provide authentication and backend functionality. These services employ security measures designed to protect the data they process. Access to user data is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide and maintain the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as it is necessary to provide the App's functionality and maintain your account.
Information associated with your account, including your email address, learning-related information, purchase information, and other data necessary to provide the App's functionality, is retained while your account remains active.
Information collected or processed by third-party services used by the App may be retained in accordance with the privacy policies and data retention practices of their respective providers.

For information about deleting your account, please refer to the Account Deletion page:
https://leptonapps.com/yteng-acc-deletion

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16 without appropriate parental consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information from our records as soon as practicable.
If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section of this Privacy Policy.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us. You can reach us by email at support@leptonapps.com. We will do our best to address your concerns and respond to your inquiries.
