Account Deletion

You can delete your Think English account in two ways: in the app or by email.

In the app

1- Open the app.
2- Go to Profile from the bottom menu.
3- Tap Delete account.
4- Type DELETE to enable the delete button.
5- Tap Delete to confirm.

By email

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to support@leptonapps.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within a few days.
Deleting your account will permanently remove your account, progress, and remaining credits. This action cannot be undone.
